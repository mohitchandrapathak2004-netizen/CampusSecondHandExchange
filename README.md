Project Overview: Campus Exchange
1. Objective
The Campus Exchange project aims to develop a secure, peer-to-peer mobile marketplace specifically designed for the students of Graphic Era Hill University. The application serves as a centralized digital platform where students can seamlessly buy, sell, and exchange second-hand academic resources, electronics, and daily essentials within a trusted campus community.

2. Problem Statement
College students frequently need to purchase textbooks, lab equipment, and hostel essentials, which are often expensive when bought new. While students graduate or move to new semesters, they are left with usable items they no longer need. Existing public marketplaces lack the localized focus and security required by students, making peer-to-peer trading difficult and prone to trust issues.

3. The Solution
Campus Exchange solves this by providing an exclusive, domain-restricted ecosystem. By enforcing registration strictly through the official @gehu.ac.in university email domain, the app guarantees that all buyers and sellers are verified peers. This high-trust environment encourages sustainable reuse of goods, helps students save money, and fosters campus connectivity.

4. Technology Stack

Frontend: Native Android (Java & XML)

Backend Database: Firebase Cloud Firestore (NoSQL real-time database)

Authentication: Firebase Auth (Email/Password with domain validation)

Media Storage: Cloudinary (Direct-to-cloud image hosting)

Image Rendering: Glide API (for asynchronous image fetching and caching)

5. Core Features

Secure Onboarding: Domain-validated registration and login system.

Dynamic Home Feed: A global marketplace dashboard showcasing all available items with high-quality cached images.

Seamless Listing Creation: An intuitive form for users to upload photos directly from their gallery, set prices, and categorize their items.

Personalized Dashboard: A "My Listings" section allowing users to track and manage their active inventory.

Instant Communication: A direct-to-dialer integration that allows buyers to contact sellers instantly via their native phone app, removing the friction of complex in-app messaging.

6. Target Audience
The primary users of this application are the active student body of Graphic Era Hill University, specifically catering to those looking for cost-effective academic supplies and those looking to declutter and monetize their unused items.
